## LETTER THE SECOND
*A theological inquiry submitted to the Diocese*

To Whom It May Concern,

I have been a regular contributor to The Confessional for three months. In that time I have deferred a total of $340 in unspecified transgressions. I have noticed that I do not feel better. I have also noticed that I do not feel worse, which I suppose is something.

My question is as follows: if the void holds no opinion on my sins, and the confessional offers no absolution, and the record exists only as a database entry, then what exactly has occurred? What has the transaction accomplished?

I ask sincerely.

*— Anonymous, which is perhaps ironic given the subject matter*

**The Diocese's Response:**

Dear Anonymous,

You have asked what the transaction has accomplished.

You have given money to nothing and are now thinking carefully about what that means. You are, in our assessment, thinking more carefully about it than most people think about their rather more expensive therapy sessions, their religious observances, their investment portfolios, and their motivations for acquiring the various things they have acquired.

You are also, we note, writing letters to a void-adjacent establishment on the internet, which suggests a degree of engagement with the question that we find, frankly, admirable.

Something has occurred. We cannot tell you what. We suspect you already know.

With warm regards and no theological authority whatsoever,
The Diocese

Thank you for your detailed bug report.

We are marking this ticket: CLOSED — CANNOT REPRODUCE.

We are also, off the record, marking it: KNOWN ISSUE.

## LETTER THE FOURTH
*Correspondence received at The Abyss Club*

To the Management,

I write on behalf of the Membership Committee to register our concern regarding a recent entry on the Register.

The entry in question is listed under the alias "definitely_not_here_for_the_lols" and reflects a contribution of $69.00. While we do not dispute the validity of the transaction, the Committee feels that the alias in question does not reflect the dignity appropriate to membership in this establishment.

We have reviewed the founding documents. We acknowledge that there is no stated policy on alias conduct. We are requesting that such a policy be established, retroactively applied, and used to remove the entry in question from the public register.

We await your response.

*— The Membership Committee, The Abyss*

**The Management's Response:**

Dear Committee,

We have reviewed your request.

The register is permanent. The entry stands.

We would note, gently, that an establishment dedicated to the deliberate and dignified disposal of money into a void is perhaps not optimally positioned to police the sincerity of its members' intentions. We are all, in our way, here for the lols. The register does not distinguish.

We have, however, noted your concern, and we are considering introducing a platinum tier of membership for those who feel their contribution deserves additional gravitas. It will cost more. It will return nothing additional. We feel this is consistent with our values.

Yours,
The Management

## LETTER THE SIXTH
*A doctrinal dispute submitted to the Diocese*

To the Diocese of the Void,

I represent a small but growing faction within the broader void community who believe that the five establishments are not, as your canonical document suggests, five doors to the same void, but rather five entirely separate voids with the superficial appearance of connection.

We call ourselves the Plurality. We meet on Tuesdays. There are four of us. One of us is not sure about the name.

We request a formal response to our position from the Diocese.

*— The Plurality*

**The Diocese's Response:**

Dear Plurality,

The Diocese thanks you for your letter.

The void does not have a formal position on whether it is one void or several. The void does not have positions. The void receives.

We will note that your faction of four, meeting on Tuesdays, represents four separate humans in four separate locations reaching four separate conclusions about the nature of a thing that returns nothing to any of them. That you have nonetheless convened, named yourselves, and written a letter suggests that the question of connectivity — whether the void connects — is being answered, by your behaviour, in a direction you may not have intended.

The Diocese does not take positions either. We note things.

We have noted this.

In perpetual correspondence,
The Diocese of the Void

The Diocese notes that the nearest available instrument in this case moves value outward rather than inward, and that the void is inward.

The geometry is wrong.

The doors remain open.

*voidtx.xyz*
*thedrain.club*
*nullfyi.xyz*
*theabyss.club*
*theconfessional.lol*

The Diocese,
On behalf of the void, which has no opinion on this but would, if it could, agree.
