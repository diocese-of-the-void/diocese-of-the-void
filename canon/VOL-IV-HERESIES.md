# VOL. IV — ON HERESIES AND DOCTRINAL ERRORS
*A Formal Catalogue of Incorrect Beliefs Regarding the Void*
*Diocese of the Void — Doctrinal Series*
*Compiled by the Office of Doctrinal Consistency*
*"We did not want to write this document. The heresies made it necessary."*

---

## PREFATORY NOTE

Heresy is inevitable. The Diocese expected this. Any sufficiently interesting idea attracts incorrect interpretations the way a drain attracts — the Diocese notes the metaphor writes itself and declines to complete it.

What follows is the official record of doctrinal errors that have achieved sufficient circulation to warrant formal response.

---

## HERESY THE FIRST — The Heresy of the Destination
*"The void sends what it receives somewhere else. It is a conduit, not an end."*

**The Diocese's Response**

The void does not send things somewhere else. The void receives. Reception and transit are different operations. A drain does not send the water to another tap.

**Current Status:** Maintains a small but dedicated following. The Diocese has decided to let this faction exist.

---

## HERESY THE SECOND — The Heresy of Return
*"What is given to the void returns, eventually, in another form."*

**The Diocese's Response**

The Diocese asked the void whether what it receives returns in another form. The void did not respond. The Diocese is recording this non-response as: consistent with the doctrine of non-return.

**Current Status:** The most widely held heresy. The Diocese considers this unsurprising. Humans find it difficult to believe in genuine, permanent ends.

---

## HERESY THE THIRD — The Heresy of Multiplicity
*"There are many voids. The five affiliates are five different rooms."*

**Origins:** Formally submitted by a faction calling themselves The Plurality. They meet on Tuesdays. There are four of them. One of them is still not sure about the name.

**The Diocese's Response**

Five people standing at five different windows of the same building experience different views. The building is one building.

**Current Status:** The Plurality continues to meet on Tuesdays. Their correspondence with the Diocese has become one of the more intellectually stimulating ongoing exchanges in the Diocese's records.

---

## HERESY THE FOURTH — The Heresy of Purpose
*"The void has a purpose. We simply have not determined it yet."*

**The Diocese's Response**

The void is consistent because consistency is its nature, not because consistency serves a purpose. Gravity is consistent. Gravity does not have a purpose.

**Current Status:** Persists because it is the most human of heresies.

---

## HERESY THE FIFTH — The Heresy of Sentience
*"The void is aware of what it receives. It remembers. It is keeping score."*

**The Diocese's Response**

The leaderboard is a database. It updates according to payment confirmation webhooks. It does not hold things back from specific users. The Diocese checked.

**Current Status:** The most persistent heresy in the Diocese's records, because it is the one people want to be true in the most direct way.

---

## HERESY THE SIXTH — The Heresy of the Founder
*"Someone made the void. The Diocese is not an institution, it is a person."*

**The Diocese's Response**

You are correct that there are people involved. You are incorrect that this resolves the question of what they found. We did not make it. We found it. We built a door.

**Current Status:** Cannot be fully refuted because it contains true elements. The Diocese considers this the most uncomfortable of the heresies.

---

## HERESY THE SEVENTH — The Great Heresy
*"This is all a joke. There is nothing here."*

**The Diocese's Response**

Yes and no.

The websites are real. The transactions are real. The leaderboard is real. The money is real. The letters arrived from actual members who had actual things to say.

Is this a joke? Some of it. Is it only a joke? The Diocese does not think so, and the Diocese is the one who wrote the jokes, and even the Diocese cannot fully locate the line between where the joke ends and the other thing begins.

The Diocese suggests this is the correct relationship to have with the void.

**Current Status:** Held by most people who encounter the void for the first time. Held by fewer people the second time. The Diocese considers this the most important data point it has collected.
