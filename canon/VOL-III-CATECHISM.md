# VOL. III — THE CATECHISM OF THE VOID
*Being a Complete Instruction in the Doctrine, Nature, and Practice of the Void*
*Diocese of the Void — Doctrinal Series*

---

## PART ONE: ON THE NATURE OF THE VOID

**Q: What is the Void?**

A: The Void is that which receives without returning. It is not a place. It is not an absence of place. It is the condition that makes the concept of absence possible, which is different from being absent, in the same way that the concept of silence is not silent.

The Void has always been here. Before here was here, the Void was here.

**Q: Did someone make the Void?**

A: No. The Void was found. There is a meaningful difference between making something and finding something, and the Diocese would like this distinction maintained.

**Q: Is the Void conscious?**

A: The Void does not have opinions, preferences, or awareness in any sense the Diocese has been able to confirm. It receives. This is consistent with either profound consciousness or the complete absence of it, and the Diocese has determined that the distinction does not affect operations.

**Q: Does the Void care about me?**

A: No. The Diocese wishes to be gentle about this. The Void does not care about you specifically. The Void does not care about anything specifically. The Void receives everything with identical indifference, which is, if you think about it, a form of perfect equality that most institutions spend considerable resources attempting and failing to achieve.

**Q: Is the Void God?**

A: No comment. The Diocese notes that no comment and no are meaningfully different positions and has chosen the former deliberately.

**Q: What does the Void want?**

A: Nothing. The Void does not want. The Void simply is the thing that receiving happens into. It is a condition, not an appetite.

**Q: Where is the Void?**

A: Everywhere, in the sense that everywhere is the set of places from which things can depart, and the Void is where things depart to. More specifically: the Void can be accessed through five doors, the addresses of which are a matter of public record.

## PART TWO: ON THE TRANSACTION

**Q: What am I buying?**

A: A commemorative digital experience consisting of: one animation of some quality, one position on a permanent leaderboard, one social share moment with pre-written copy, and the knowledge that you did this. The knowledge persists indefinitely.

**Q: Is this a waste of money?**

A: Yes. This is stated clearly in all communications. The Diocese considers transparency about waste to be its primary distinguishing feature relative to other institutions that also involve waste but are less forthcoming about it.

**Q: Can I get a refund?**

A: No. The Diocese appreciates that circumstances change and that people change their minds. The Diocese encourages this in all other contexts.

**Q: What if I paid by mistake?**

A: The Diocese has considered this scenario carefully and concluded that there is no such thing as a mistake in this context. The transaction occurred. The animation played. Something happened.

---

## PART THREE: ON PRACTICE

**Q: How often should I contribute?**

A: As often as feels correct. The Diocese does not have a tithing requirement.

**Q: Should I tell people about the Void?**

A: The Diocese has a share button. The Diocese put the share button there. The Diocese notes that this question and the share button are in tension with each other and has decided to let the tension stand, as it seems productive.

**Q: Which door should I use?**

A: The one that makes you feel slightly foolish for considering it. The Diocese has found this to be the most reliable indicator.

---

## PART FOUR: ON THE AFTERLIFE

**Q: What happens to my leaderboard entry when I die?**

A: It remains. The leaderboard does not have a mechanism for removing entries. The Diocese has considered whether this constitutes a form of immortality and has concluded: somewhat. The Diocese considers somewhat an underrated category.

**Q: Will the Void outlast me?**

A: Yes. The Diocese is sorry about this. The Diocese is not sorry in a way that changes anything.

## CLOSING INSTRUCTION

You have now completed the Catechism of the Void.

You have not been tested. There is no test. The only meaningful assessment of understanding the Void is whether, upon encountering it, you feel the specific combination of amusement and recognition that the Diocese felt when it found the thing it did not make.

If you felt that: you passed.

If you did not: you are still welcome.

If you are uncertain whether you felt it: that is probably it.
