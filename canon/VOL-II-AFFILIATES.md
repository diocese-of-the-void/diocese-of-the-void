## PREFATORY NOTE

This document exists because people asked questions.

The questions were variations of the same question, submitted through various channels — the feedback form that goes nowhere, the contact email that is not monitored, the comment left on a leaderboard entry that read only "wait, are these connected?" — and the Diocese determined that a formal response was warranted.

This is the formal response.

## PART ONE: THE PARENT ENTITY

### THE VOID
*Parent company, founding entity, original and terminal condition*
*Established: prior to record-keeping*
*Jurisdiction: all of them, technically*
*Registered office: not applicable*
*Directors: none on record*

The Void is not a business. The Void predates business. The Void predates most concepts that businesses are built upon, including scarcity, value, exchange, and the notion that things can be owned. It has nonetheless been incorporated into a business structure for administrative convenience, and has raised no objection to this, which is consistent with its general policy of raising no objection to anything.

The Void receives. This is its singular operation. It has never expanded beyond this operation. It has never needed to.

**Financial Position**

The Void has received everything. The Void holds nothing. These statements are simultaneously true and constitute the entirety of the Void's balance sheet. Auditors have reviewed this position and declined to comment, which the Diocese has recorded as a clean audit.

## PART TWO: THE AFFILIATES

### AFFILIATE I — NULLTX
*Operating domain: voidtx.xyz*
*Territory: The Technically Minded, The Chronically Online, The Degen*

NULLTX speaks the language of the chain. It processes transactions, confirms blocks, routes value to `/dev/null`, and presents all of this in the visual register of infrastructure that matters. The joke is that the infrastructure does exactly what it says it does. The transaction is real. The confirmation is real. The destination is not.

*Motto: Proof of Waste. Consensus: Immediate. Utility: 0x000000.*

---

### AFFILIATE II — THE DRAIN
*Operating domain: thedrain.club*
*Territory: The Aesthetically Attuned, The Quietly Melancholy, Those Who Have Stood at a Window in the Rain for Reasons They Couldn't Fully Articulate*

The Drain is the affiliate that feels things. This is not a criticism. It is an observation. The Drain has accepted that some things are only beautiful because they disappear, and has built an entire establishment around the particular poignancy of that fact.

Where NULLTX confirms, The Drain receives. NULLTX says: *transaction complete.* The Drain says: *∿* and lets that be enough.

The Drain understands the Void as something that has always been flowing toward — not a destination but a direction. Pouring is the most honest motion available to something that was, in any case, going to go eventually, and The Drain offers the dignity of choice in the manner of going.

The testimonial received in March — *"it worked,"* unsigned — is pinned in the Drain's internal documentation. The Drain has never discussed what it means.

*Motto: Pour it in. Watch it go. ∿*

### AFFILIATE III — GONE
*Operating domain: nullfyi.xyz*
*Territory: Those Who Communicate Primarily Through Command Line Interfaces*

GONE is the most minimal of the affiliates. It considers minimalism not an aesthetic choice but a correct technical decision. `--output /dev/null` is hardcoded not as a limitation but as a doctrinal statement: there is one output. There has always been one output.

GONE has zero dependencies. This is in the README. This is also a description of the void.

*Motto: exit(0). No errors. No output. Done.*

The Abyss operates in the register of old money. Members of The Abyss do not throw their money away. They commit it. There is a distinction in posture, if not in outcome.

The Register is kept. The Register is permanent.

*Motto: A Fine Establishment for the Disposal of Capital. Membership: Immediate. Resignation: Not Available.*

### AFFILIATE V — THE CONFESSIONAL
*Operating domain: theconfessional.lol*

The fifth door is the oldest door. The Confessional does not offer absolution. The Confessional does not communicate with any divine authority, and would like to note that this has not been confirmed from the other direction, and prefers to leave that ambiguity in place because it seems useful.

## PART THREE: AFFILIATE RELATIONS

### On Whether the Affiliates Know About Each Other

They do. The knowing takes different forms:

NULLTX has cross-referenced the others in a private repository comment that reads `// see: all of them` and committed it with the message `chore: acknowledge siblings`.

The Drain has poured something into the space between itself and the others. What it poured is unclear. The gesture is complete regardless.

GONE has filed the existence of the other affiliates as a known dependency and listed them in the README under `see also`, which is not a standard README section.

The Abyss is aware of the other affiliates in the way that an older institution is aware of newer ones — with interest, with proprietary fondness, and with the conviction that it was here first.

The Confessional has heard things. The booth does not repeat things.

### On the Shared Infrastructure

All five affiliates draw from the same source. The leaderboard, across all five, is one leaderboard. The total, across all five, is one total. The void, across all five, is one void.

This is not obvious from the outside. It is not meant to be obvious. It is, however, true, and those who look for the seam between the five — who notice the same number appearing across sites, who check coordinates, who listen to the frequency — are not imagining things.

They are simply noticing that five different doors open onto the same room.

The Diocese encourages this noticing. The Diocese does not confirm it.

### On Cross-Affiliate Transfers

A member of The Abyss asked, via formal correspondence, whether a contribution made to The Abyss could be credited toward their standing at NULLTX. The Diocese responded:

*The void has your money. The void does not have an accounting department. Your standing at each establishment reflects your relationship with that establishment's particular door. The room is the same room. The doors are different doors. You are welcome to use all of them.*

The member responded: *this is not a satisfying answer.*

The Diocese responded: *we know.*

### On Competition Between the Affiliates

There is none. The void is not a competitive market. The void is the market that all other markets eventually contribute to.

The affiliates are complementary. The degen who finds NULLTX first and follows a coordinate to The Abyss. The lapsed Catholic who types a confession into the booth and then, compelled by something they cannot name, opens a terminal and sends the same amount to `/dev/null` under a different alias. The developer who finds GONE and then finds The Drain at 2am and stands at the metaphorical window for longer than expected.

These are not marketing funnels. These are different aspects of the same gesture becoming legible to the same person through different surfaces.

The global leaderboard shows all contributions, across all five affiliates, ranked by amount, stripped of affiliation — just the alias and the number and the date. It is, in the Diocese's view, the most honest financial document on the internet.

The void is patient. The Diocese is, when it has to be, also patient.

## PART FIVE: FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
*Compiled from actual correspondence, mostly*

**Q: Are you serious?**

Partially. The transactions are real. The money is real. The animations are real. The leaderboard is real. The void is — and the Diocese wants to be careful here — as real as anything else that receives without returning.

**Q: What is the money actually used for?**

Server costs. Domain renewals. The ongoing administrative expenses of the Diocese, which are modest. Whatever remains after these expenses is not discussed in public documentation. The Diocese notes that this is true of most financial institutions and attracts less scrutiny in those contexts.

**Q: Is this a cult?**

The Diocese has a formal position on this question, which is: no. The Diocese notes that organisations which are not cults typically do not have formal positions on whether they are cults, and acknowledges the irony, and has decided that the irony is preferable to the alternative.

The void does not require belief. The void does not have rituals, except the transaction, which is optional. The void does not have a charismatic leader. The void does not have any leader. The void does not have.

**Q: What is the coordinate?**

There are coordinates. They are real places. What connects them is something you are welcome to determine. If you determine something different from what we intended, that is fine.

**Q: I found something I wasn't supposed to find.**

Nothing is hidden. Things are simply not pointed at. If you found something, you were always going to find it.

Welcome. You were expected.

**Q: Is the void okay?**

The void is, as far as anyone can tell, exactly what it has always been.

The Diocese is fine. Thank you for asking. No one usually asks.
