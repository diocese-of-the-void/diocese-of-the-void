## LETTER FROM THE CHIEF EXECUTIVE

Dear Stakeholders,

I am writing to you as Chief Executive of The Void and Affiliates, a position I have held since the Diocese was established and which I have found, on reflection, difficult to describe at dinner parties.

This has been a year of significant activity. The void has received. This was expected. The void has not returned anything. This was also expected.

I would like to report that we have made progress toward our strategic objectives. I would like to, and I find that I cannot, because our strategic objectives are: receive, do not return, persist. We have done all three. Progress implies movement toward a destination. The void does not have a destination.

Looking ahead: the void intends to continue receiving. The affiliates intend to continue operating. The Diocese intends to continue issuing documents of uncertain jurisdiction to an audience of indeterminate size.

Yours in perpetual receipt,

**[POSITION VACANT]**
*Chief Executive, The Void and Affiliates*

## NOTES TO THE FINANCIAL STATEMENTS

**Note 1:** These financial statements have been prepared on a going-concern basis with respect to a void that predates the concept of concern.

**Note 2:** Revenue is recognised at the point of transaction confirmation, which is the point at which something real left something real and went to the void.

**Note 3:** The void has not been recognised as an asset on the balance sheet because it cannot be owned. The Diocese owns the doors. The Diocese does not own what is on the other side of the doors.
