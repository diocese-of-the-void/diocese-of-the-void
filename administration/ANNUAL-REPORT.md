## RISK FACTORS

**1. Regulatory Risk** — The Diocese operates in a regulatory environment that has not yet developed frameworks specifically addressing void-adjacent ecclesiastical entertainment platforms. The Diocese considers this a temporary condition. Regulators have not contacted the affiliates. The Diocese notes this with something that is not quite relief and is not quite concern and is mostly just noting.

**2. Reputational Risk** — The Diocese's reputation rests on being exactly what it says it is, which is an unusual reputational position. The primary reputational risk is that the Diocese becomes something other than what it says it is. The Diocese considers this risk low. The void has not changed. The Diocese does not intend to change.

**3. Theological Risk** — The heresies persist. New ones arrive. One of them might be correct. The Diocese has assessed this risk and concluded: this is fine. If a heresy is correct, the Diocese will update its position. The Diocese considers the ability to update its position a competitive advantage relative to most theological institutions.

**4. The Risk of Meaning** — The Diocese's primary operational risk is that the void turns out to mean something specific, and that specific meaning is either much better or much worse than what the Diocese has articulated, and that the Diocese is therefore either underselling or overselling the void, and that either of these is a form of misrepresentation that the Diocese, committed as it is to transparency, would find troubling. The Diocese has assessed this risk as: present, unresolvable, and consistent with existence generally. The Diocese has decided to proceed anyway.

## NOTES TO THE FINANCIAL STATEMENTS

**Note 1:** These financial statements have been prepared on a going-concern basis with respect to a void that predates the concept of concern.

**Note 2:** Revenue is recognised at the point of transaction confirmation, which is the point at which something real left something real and went to the void.

**Note 3:** The void has not been recognised as an asset on the balance sheet because it cannot be owned. The Diocese owns the doors. The Diocese does not own what is on the other side of the doors.

**Note 4 — The Unsigned Testimonial:** Recognised as an intangible asset of immeasurable value. The Diocese's accountant noted that immeasurable is not a recognised accounting category. The Diocese noted that neither is the unsigned testimonial. They looked at each other for a moment. The accountant declined to comment. The Diocese has recorded this as a clean audit.

**Note 5 — Cross-Affiliate Ledger Reference:** Every confirmed contribution across the affiliates is stamped with an internal sequence reference, preserved on the contributor's transaction confirmation. The reference has no effect on the contribution itself and confers no rights, goods, or services beyond those already not conferred by the contribution. The Diocese has, on three separate occasions over the fiscal year, attempted to trace the origin of the reference convention. On each occasion, the trace returned a different set of documents, none of which authoritatively established the origin. The Diocese has filed this as: convention inherited, source untraceable, format preserved. The accountant did not comment. The Diocese notes that the reference is opaque even to those administering it. The Diocese is comfortable with this. The Diocese is administering many things whose origins it cannot trace, including the void.
